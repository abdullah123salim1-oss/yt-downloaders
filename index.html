Frequently asked questions

Helpful notes about supported links and temporary service limits.

Why might a video not load?

Some videos are private, age-restricted, unavailable in your region, or temporarily limited by YouTube. Wait before trying again and use YouTube's own options where available. This tool does not bypass those restrictions.

What can I download?

Only download videos you own or have permission to save, and follow YouTube's Terms of Service and applicable copyright laws.

Why are some quality or audio options missing?

The options shown depend on the formats YouTube makes available for that video at the time of the request.
